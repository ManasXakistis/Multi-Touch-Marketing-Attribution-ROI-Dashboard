Part 1: The KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)
KPIs are just numbers that tell a business how well it's performing. Think of them as a "report card." Here are the four you need to calculate:

Total Spend — Simply how much money was spent on ads in total.
CPC (Cost Per Click) — If you spent ₹1000 on ads and 200 people clicked, your CPC = ₹1000 ÷ 200 = ₹5. It tells you how expensive each click was.

CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) — How much money was spent to get one paying customer. If ₹10,000 was spent and 50 people bought something → CAC = ₹200.

ROAS (Return On Ad Spend) — For every ₹1 spent on ads, how much revenue came back? If you spent ₹1000 and earned ₹4000 → ROAS = 4x. Higher is better.

Part 2: Star Schema (Data Modeling)
This is how you organize the data in a database. Here's a diagram to make it intuitive: The Fact table sits in the center — it holds all the numbers (spend, clicks, revenue). The surrounding Dimension tables hold descriptive context (who, when, where, which campaign). Together they form a star shape — hence the name.
